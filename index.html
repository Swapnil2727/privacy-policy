ScanSign — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Applies to the ScanSign Android app

Overview

ScanSign is a document scanner and PDF signer that is designed to be local-first. Your documents, scans, and signatures are stored on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

Data stored on your device

  • Documents: scanned pages, PDF files, and thumbnails are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Signatures: drawn and image signatures you save are stored on your device.
  • Preferences: theme and scan-quality settings are stored on your device.

You can delete any of this data at any time by deleting documents or signatures inside the app, clearing the app's data in system settings, or uninstalling the app.

Camera and scanning

ScanSign uses the camera only to scan documents. Page detection and image processing happen entirely on your device (via Google's ML Kit on-device SDK). Photos you take or import for scanning are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted anywhere.

Crash reporting

ScanSign uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to collect information about app crashes, such as stack traces, app version, device model, and operating system version. This helps us find and fix bugs. Crash reports do not contain your documents or their contents.

Usage statistics

ScanSign uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect basic, anonymous usage information such as which screens are opened and how often features are used. This data cannot be used to identify you personally and never includes the contents of your documents.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, email address, or personal details are collected.
  • No document content, scanned images, or signature images leave your device.
  • No location data, contacts, or device identifiers beyond those described above.
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs beyond Firebase (no ads in this version).

Data sharing

Crash and usage data described above is processed by Google LLC (Firebase) on our behalf for app stability and improvement. It is not sold, and it is not shared with any other third party.

Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Crash and usage data: retained by Google according to Firebase's retention policies (typically 14 days for analytics event data, 90 days for crash data, aggregated).

Children

ScanSign is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
